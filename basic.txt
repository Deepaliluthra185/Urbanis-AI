PART 4: TOKEN REDUCTION & DEDUPLICATION IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Applied Core Optimizations in backend/agents.py & backend/server.py:
  1. Zero Thinking Budget (thinking_budget=0):
     - Eliminated 60-150 hidden reasoning tokens that Gemini 2.5 Flash outputs
       internally before writing text.
  2. Strict max_output_tokens Caps (capped to <=60):
     - Cut autoregressive token generation iterations from ~250+ down to ~55-60.
     - Direct 77-80% reduction in decoding FLOPs and energy.
  3. Prompt Token Pruning:
     - Stripped conversational fluff, repeated guidance, and redundant filler.
  4. Low Temperature (0.15):
     - Ensures deterministic, high-probability tokens; avoids rambling outputs.

PART 10: SHAPELY 2.X WITH C-GEOS & STRTREE SPATIAL INDEXING
================================================================================
Problem:
  Brute-force iterating through hundreds of GeoJSON seismic, river floodplain, and
  conservation polygon coordinates in pure Python wastes severe CPU cycles (O(N) complexity)
  and introduces seconds of latency per parcel query.

Solution:
  Integrated Shapely 2.2.0 compiled with native C-GEOS (v3.14.1) utilizing an
  O(log N) Sort-Tile-Recursive Tree (STRtree) spatial index in backend/spatial_index.py.

Architecture & Datasets:
  - C-GEOS STRtree Index: Compiles 69+ detailed national river floodplains, High Flood
    Level (HFL) setback buffers, municipal conservation reserves, and Indian Seismic
    Zones into an in-memory R-tree.
  - Indian Seismic Atlas (IS 1893:2016 Part 1):
      * Zone V  (Z=0.36): Kashmir, HP/UK High Himalayas, NE India, Kutch, North Bihar.
      * Zone IV (Z=0.24): Delhi NCR, Northern Punjab (Amritsar, Jalandhar), Koyna.
      * Zone III(Z=0.16): Mumbai MMR, Pune, Gujarat Mainland, Kerala.
      * Zone II (Z=0.10): Peninsular Shield (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai).
  - River Hazards: 23 Pan-India River Corridors (CWC National Flood Atlas) + 7 Regional
    drainage networks + dynamic user-uploaded GIS GeoJSON layers.
  - Regulatory Enforcement:
      * Intersects Flood / River Buffer -> Statutory HARD VETO (Bylaw MB-22.7).
      * Intersects Ecological Reserve   -> Statutory HARD VETO (Bylaw MB-14.2).
      * Seismic Zone Mapped             -> Automated Ductile Requirements (IS 13920:2016).
